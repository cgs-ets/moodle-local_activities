Health and Medical
Medical Issues - Child Illness - exisiting medical condition requiring assistance while on the excursion 2 Review all children’s Medical Conditions prior to departure. If any child is prone to travel sickness, ensure that they are seated at the front of the bus and ensure first aid kits have sick bags stocked in them. Ensure the excursion bag is packed with suitable first aid kit and resources to accommodate children’s illness or medical conditions. If needed, meet with the Health Clinic to discuss any medical flags accordingly.  5 Staff in Charge & Accompanying Staff Before the Excursion 
Incorrect management of medication 2 If a student requires medication while on the excursion, updated Medication Treatment Advice Forms must be provided by parents prior to departure. Staff to check the form provided by parents to ensure details and dosage required are correct and confirmed. School nurses to provide support and advice as required. Medications must be in their original packaging. Medication to be managed by CGS Staff and documentmented throughout the programme. Medication double check by staff prior to administration. 5 Staff in Charge & Accompanying Staff
Child Anaphylactic Diagnosed and Undiagnosed Conditions - anaphylactic emergency while on excursion 2 Review all children’s medical plan requirements prior to the excursion. Ensure all identified children have their own personal auto injectors/Ventolin/medication on the excursion. Spare auto injectors and Ventolin to be packed in the excursion bag. When a medical episode occurs, an incident report is to be completed online through ‘Log an incident’ button on CGS Connect. Families to be notified of incident/illness/injury/trauma as per the School's notification process. Accompanying staff made aware of students with anaphylactic or asthma conditions prior to departure. 4 Staff in Charge & Accompanying Staff Before and During the Excursion 
Choking while eating  3 Ensure all children are supervised and remain seated while eating. Accompanying staff to have current First Aid and CPR certifications. 5 Staff in Charge & Accompanying Staff Whilst eating and drinking
